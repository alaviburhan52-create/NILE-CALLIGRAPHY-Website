Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

From choosing an original artwork to framing, customization and delivery, find answers to the questions our collectors ask most often.

About Our Artwork

Are your paintings original?

Yes. Our original paintings are individually handcrafted artworks created as unique pieces. Availability depends on the specific artwork.

Are the artworks handmade?

Our original works are created through handcrafted artistic processes with careful attention to calligraphy, composition and detail.

What type of Islamic calligraphy do you create?

Our collection focuses on Islamic calligraphy, Qur'anic verses, sacred names and other works inspired by Islamic heritage and artistic tradition.

Orders & Customization

Can I request a custom artwork?

Yes. Custom commissions can be discussed based on the desired text, size, style, colours, framing and intended space.

Can I request a specific size?

Size options depend on the artwork. For custom commissions, we can discuss dimensions according to your space and requirements.

Can I choose the frame?

Framing options can be discussed when placing an order. Availability may vary depending on the artwork and required finish.

Delivery & Payment

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes. Delivery arrangements can be discussed for customers across Uganda. Delivery time and cost depend on the destination and artwork.

How is my artwork packaged?

Original artworks are prepared carefully for transport with protection appropriate to the artwork and framing requirements.

How do I pay for an artwork?

Payment arrangements are confirmed directly with our team when your order is being finalized.

Before You Purchase

Can I see more pictures of an artwork?

Yes. If you are interested in a particular artwork, contact us and we can provide additional information and available images.

Can I physically view an artwork before buying?

Viewing arrangements depend on the artwork's location and availability. Contact us to discuss the available options.

Do you accept mosque, hotel, school or corporate commissions?

Yes. We welcome suitable institutional and commissioned projects. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

Still Have A Question?

If you cannot find the answer you're looking for, speak directly with our team about an artwork, custom commission or delivery.
